# Product Backend V1.1.2 — Final Backend Before Frontend

This update completes the backend contract required by the EyeAI frontend.

## Assistant corrections

- Qwen now returns plain text only; backend-owned structured fields are never generated by the LLM.
- Malformed JSON, truncated output, unsupported claims, and missing citations trigger a deterministic grounded fallback.
- Arabic terminology is fixed to `التنكس البقعي المرتبط بالعمر`.
- Inline RAG citations use backend-assigned numbers and cannot be invented by Qwen.
- The response now includes:
  - `question_route`
  - `clinical_interpretation`
  - `patient_evidence`
  - `heatmap_spatial`
  - `technical_review_profile`
  - `references`
  - `limitations`
  - `grounding`

## Heatmap intelligence

The backend calculates on CPU:

- Peak pixel and normalized coordinate.
- Attribution centroid.
- Deterministic 3x3 spatial labels.
- Dominant attribution region and mass fraction.
- High-focus bounding box.
- TTA explanation similarity.

The assistant receives calculated values only. It does not infer anatomy from the image.

## Approved-section RAG

- `knowledge_base/reference_manifest.yaml` defines allowed topics and excluded headings.
- NICE and AAO filenames downloaded by the project are supported directly.
- Beckman classification remains optional.
- Notebook 14 creates a page-level audit before indexing.
- Only selected pages are chunked and embedded.
- Retrieval is topic-routed and limited to three chunks with at most one chunk per source.
- Query embeddings stay on CPU.

## GPU and Kaggle

- RETFound explanation runs before lazy Qwen loading in the final smoke notebook.
- The shared GPU lock prevents concurrent RETFound gradients and Qwen generation.
- Qwen remains 4-bit with a 5 GiB GPU cap.
- CUDA cache is released after explanation and generation.
