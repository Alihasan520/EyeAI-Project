# EyeAI Frontend Batch 04 - Clinical Copilot and Professional PDF Reports

## Frontend

- Replaces the Clinical Assistant placeholder with a patient-, eye-, and visit-grounded workspace.
- Adds protected conversation creation, conversation history, structured assistant responses, and suggested prompts.
- Adds an animated circular thinking indicator with retrieval, evidence-review, and response-drafting stages.
- Displays patient evidence, clinical interpretation, limitations, suggested review, and approved references.
- Adds clickable reference metadata dialogs supplied directly by the RAG backend.
- Adds a reports workspace with analyzed-visit selection, optional AI report draft, PDF generation, preview, download, and report registry.
- Adds direct actions from the retinal analysis result to Clinical Copilot and Clinical Reports.

## Backend API 3.3.0

- Adds `GET /api/v1/reports` with optional `patient_id` filtering.
- Extends `POST /api/v1/visits/{visit_ref}/reports` with optional English clinical summary and approved reference metadata.
- Replaces the basic report with a professionally designed English A4 PDF.
- The PDF includes the EyeAI logo, readable identifiers, model score and threshold, original image, heatmap overlay, spatial metrics, doctor notes, approved references, model version, page footer, and clinical safety notice.

## Safety

- The assistant remains an AI-assisted review tool and does not diagnose or prescribe.
- Citation metadata is rendered from backend retrieval results rather than generated by the language model.
- The report uses the phrase `model score`, not confirmed disease probability.
